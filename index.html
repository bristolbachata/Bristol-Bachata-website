About Bristol Bachata

Founded over 8 years ago by Jan Tabaka, Bristol Bachata has grown into the heartbeat of the South West's latin dance scene. Recently featured on BBC One and BBC Four, we pride ourselves on delivering top-tier dance education in an environment that feels like home.

We specialize in Sensual Bachata, Moderna, Dominican, and Bachazouk influences. But more than just steps, we teach connection.

Our community is built on a foundation of inclusivity. Whether you're an absolute beginner taking your very first steps or an advanced dancer looking to refine your technique, our 11 passionate teachers are here to guide you. Come for the dance, stay for the family!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first night. Tap a question to see the answer.

Do I need a partner to join?
Not at all! Most people come on their own. In class we rotate partners, so you'll dance with lots of different people and meet the community along the way.
I've never danced before. Is this class for me?
Absolutely. We run split-level classes (Beginners, Improvers, Intermediate and Advanced) at the same time, so you learn at your own pace with a teacher guiding your level. Your first class is free if you're an absolute beginner. Just message us on WhatsApp to register.
When and where are the classes?
Classes run every Monday and Wednesday at Toto's By The River, 125 Redcliff St, Bristol, BS1 6HU. Classes are from 7:30 to 9:30 PM, followed by the party and practice social until 11:15 PM. Our Friday and Saturday parties are held at Docklands. Check the schedule above for the next date.
How much does it cost, and how do I book?
A full night (two classes plus the social) is £14, a single class plus the social is £10, and the social only is £6. You can book in advance on our online store, which is highly recommended, where monthly passes are also available. You can also pay at the door by cash or card.
What styles of bachata do you teach?
We specialise in Sensual, Moderna, Dominican and Bachazouk-influenced bachata. Our 11 teachers focus on connection and technique, so you'll build a strong foundation whatever style you end up loving most.
Is there parking, and what should I know before I arrive?
There is free parking after 6 PM on the single yellow lines nearby, spots limited.
What should I wear, and do I need special dance shoes?
No special dance shoes needed! Wear something comfortable that you can move in. For shoes, pick something comfortable with a smooth, flexible sole that lets you pivot and glide easily. Very grippy soles can be hard on your knees and ankles when turning. Some people like to dress up a little for the social, but it's completely up to you.
Do you offer monthly passes?
Yes! If you're ready to commit, monthly passes are available on our online store. They're a great way to keep improving week after week, and you can see the current options and prices there.
Do I need to pre-book, or can I pay on the door?
You can do either. Booking in advance on our online store is highly recommended, as it guarantees your place and makes for a smoother entry. If you decide last minute, you're welcome to turn up and pay at the door by cash or card.
How do I know which level to join?
We teach different levels at the same time: Beginners, Improvers, Intermediate and Advanced. If you've never danced bachata, or only done it a handful of times, start in Beginners. If you're not sure, just ask a teacher when you arrive and we'll point you to the right group. You can always move up as you progress.

Dance Floor Vibes & Etiquette

Keep it fun, safe, and respectful for everyone!

Stay Fresh

Arrive clean, with deodorant and fresh breath.

Respect the Class

Listen to teachers when they're explaining. No talking over them.

Mind the Space

Respect your partner's boundaries and personal space.

Safe Hands

Keep holds safe, respectful, and never force a move.

Meet Their Level

Don't try tricky moves or "teach" on the social floor.

Watch the Floor

Avoid collisions, and look out for others around you.

OK to Say No

If you feel uncomfortable, it's totally okay to decline a dance.

We've Got Your Back

Spot something off? Tell myself or a member of our team!
