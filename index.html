About Bristol Bachata

Founded over 8 years ago by international artist Jan Tabaka, Bristol Bachata is one of the biggest and most established bachata communities in the UK.

Whatever your level, we focus on solid fundamentals through a fun, relaxed and technical approach — taking you from Zero to Hero.

We offer a full spectrum of dance experiences: structured weekly classes, masterclasses, weekend parties, and vibrant events. Our proven method helps students build confidence and technique with real community energy.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first night. Tap a question to see the answer.

What is bachata, and what should I expect on my first night?

Bachata is a partner dance from the Dominican Republic. It is smooth, romantic and social, danced to music with a steady, easy-to-follow beat. Most people come alone and we rotate partners in class.

Do I need a partner to join?
Not at all. Most people arrive on their own and we rotate partners, so you’ll meet the community and dance with lots of different people.
I’ve never danced before. Is this class for me?
Absolutely. We run split-level classes so you learn at your own pace with teachers guiding every level.
When and where are the classes?
Classes run every Monday and Wednesday at Toto’s By The River, 125 Redcliff St, Bristol, BS1 6HU. Classes are from 7:30 to 9:30 PM.
How much does it cost, and how do I book?
A full night is £14, a single class plus social is £10, and social only is £6. You can book online or message us on WhatsApp.
What styles of bachata do you teach?
We specialise in Sensual, Moderna, Dominican and Bachazouk-influenced bachata. Our teachers focus on connection and technique, so you build a strong foundation whatever style you prefer.
