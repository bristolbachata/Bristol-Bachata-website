About

Bristol Bachata

Founded over 8 years ago by international artist Jan Tabaka, Bristol Bachata is one of the biggest and friendliest bachata communities in the UK.

Whatever your level, we focus on solid fundamentals through a fun, relaxed and technical approach — taking you from beginner to confident dancer.

We offer structured weekly classes, private coaching, parties, festivals and social events in a welcoming community atmosphere.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first night.

What is bachata, and what should I expect on my first night?
Bachata is a partner dance with roots in the Dominican Republic. It’s smooth, romantic and built around connection and rhythm. Expect a warm-up, technique-focused lesson, practice time and a relaxed social vibe.
Do I need a partner to join?
Not at all. Most people come alone. We rotate partners during class so you can meet people and learn comfortably.
I've never danced before. Is this class for me?
Absolutely. We offer split-level teaching so beginners learn at their own pace while more experienced dancers can continue progressing.
When and where are classes?
Classes run every Monday and Wednesday at Toto’s By The River, 125 Redcliff St, BS1 6HU. The class session runs from 7:30 to 9:30 PM, followed by a social practice from 9:45 PM.
How much does it cost?
A single class plus social is £10, a full night pass is £14, and social-only entry is £6. Monthly passes are also available.
Is there parking and what should I know before I arrive?
There is free parking after 6 PM on nearby single yellow lines, though spaces are limited. Wear something comfortable and bring water.
