About Bristol Bachata

Founded over 7 years ago by Jan Tabaka, Bristol Bachata has grown into the heartbeat of the South West's latin dance scene. Recently featured on BBC One and BBC Four, we pride ourselves on delivering top-tier dance education in an environment that feels like home.

We specialize in Sensual Bachata, Moderna, Dominican, and Bachazouk influences. But more than just steps, we teach connection.

Our community is built on a foundation of inclusivity. Whether you're an absolute beginner taking your very first steps or an advanced dancer looking to refine your technique, our 11 passionate teachers are here to guide you. Come for the dance, stay for the family!

Dance Floor Vibes

Keep it fun, safe, and respectful for everyone!

Stay Fresh

Arrive clean, with deodorant and fresh breath.

Respect the Class

Listen to teachers when they're explaining. No talking over them.

Mind the Space

Respect your partner's boundaries and personal space.

Safe Hands

Keep holds safe, respectful, and never force a move.

Meet Their Level

Don't try tricky moves or "teach" on the social floor.

Watch the Floor

Avoid collisions, and look out for others around you.

OK to Say No

If you feel uncomfortable, it's totally okay to decline a dance.

We've Got Your Back

Spot something off? Tell myself or a member of our team!
