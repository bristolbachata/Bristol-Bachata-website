About Bristol Bachata

Founded over 8 years ago by international artist Jan Tabaka, Bristol Bachata school (featured on BBC One) has grown into one of the most vibrant and welcoming dance communities in the South West.

From Zero to Hero. we build solid fundamentals with a fun, relaxed, yet highly technical approach. Whether you're an absolute beginner or already dancing socially, our classes are designed to help you grow with confidence.

Expect a complete dance ecosystem: friendly drop-in weekly classes, technical masterclasses, weekend parties, summer outdoor events and much more. Our proven methodology helps students improve quickly while enjoying every step of the journey.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first night. Tap a question to see the answer.

What is bachata, and what should I expect on my first night?

Bachata is a partner dance that began in the Dominican Republic. It's smooth, romantic and all about connection, danced to music with a steady, easy-to-follow beat. It's one of the most welcoming partner dances and a great social dance for beginners.

Here's how your night will go:

  • Arrive and say hello. Come a few minutes early, get settled, and meet the team.
  • Class (7:30 to 9:30 PM). We'll teach simple foundations, musicality and social dance skills.
  • Party and practice social (9:45 to 11:15 PM). This is your chance to dance, meet people and use what you've learned in a relaxed setting.

You don't need any experience, a partner or special clothes. Everyone was a beginner once, and our community loves helping newcomers feel at home.

Do I need a partner to join?
Not at all! Most people come on their own. In class we rotate partners, so you'll dance with lots of different people and meet the community along the way.
I've never danced before. Is this class for me?
Absolutely. We run split-level classes (Beginners, Improvers, Intermediate and Advanced) at the same time, so you learn at your own pace with a teacher guiding your level.
When and where are the classes?
Classes run every Monday and Wednesday at Toto's By The River, 125 Redcliff St, Bristol, BS1 6HU. Classes are from 7:30 to 9:30 PM, followed by the party and practice social until 11:15 PM.
How much does it cost, and how do I book?
A full night (two classes plus the social) is £14, a single class plus the social is £10, and the social only is £6. Booking in advance is highly recommended.
What styles of bachata do you teach?
We specialise in Sensual, Moderna, Dominican and Bachazouk-influenced bachata. Our 11 teachers focus on connection and technique, so you'll build a strong foundation whatever style you love most.
Is there parking, and what should I know before I arrive?
There is free parking after 6 PM on the single yellow lines nearby, spots limited.
What should I wear, and do I need special dance shoes?
No special dance shoes needed! Wear something comfortable that you can move in. For shoes, pick something comfortable with a smooth, flexible sole that lets you pivot and glide easily.
Do you offer monthly passes?
Yes! If you're ready to commit, monthly passes are available on our online store. They're a great way to keep improving week after week.
Do I need to pre-book, or can I pay on the door?
You can do either. Booking in advance on our online store is highly recommended, as it guarantees your place and makes for a smoother entry.
How do I know which level to join?
We teach different levels at the same time: Beginners, Improvers, Intermediate and Advanced. If you've never danced bachata, or only done it a handful of times, start in Beginners.
